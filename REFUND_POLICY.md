## 3. Claim Verification Fee - Non-Refundable

### 3.1 Why This Fee Is Non-Refundable

Once payment is received, Vukafia immediately provides:

✅ **Business Verification**
- Google Maps source verification
- Photo authenticity check
- Information validation
- Verification score assignment

✅ **Directory Listing**
- Unique business page created
- Information published on platform
- 54-nation visibility
- Search indexing and SEO

✅ **12-Month Promotion**
- Featured listing status
- WhatsApp integration setup
- Analytics dashboard access
- Seller tools and resources

✅ **Claim Processing**
- Manual review and approval
- Payment processing fees
- Administrative overhead
- Fraud prevention screening

**These services are delivered immediately upon payment. Therefore, refunds are NOT available.**

### 3.2 Non-Refundable Scenarios

Refunds will NOT be issued for:

- ❌ Change of mind after purchase
- ❌ Duplicate claim payments
- ❌ User error (wrong amount entered)
- ❌ Technical issues on buyer's device
- ❌ Claim suspension for policy violations
- ❌ Business closure or relocation
- ❌ Seller requesting removal

### 3.3 Refundable Scenarios (Rare Exceptions)

Refunds MAY be issued only in these cases:

✅ **Technical Failure by Vukafia**
- Payment processed but listing NOT created within 24 hours
- Payment duplicated (charged twice)
- System error preventing access to claimed business

✅ **Unauthorized Transaction**
- Payment made without seller's authorization
- Account compromised
- Fraudulent charge

✅ **Service Unavailability**
- Vukafia platform unavailable for 7+ consecutive days
- Seller unable to access claimed business for extended period
- Major technical outage affecting service delivery

**Proof Required**: Documentation of the issue, screenshots, transaction details

## 4. Refund Request Process

### 4.1 How to Request a Refund

**Step 1**: Gather documentation
- Transaction ID / Receipt
- Payment date
- Business ID
- Reason for refund request

**Step 2**: Submit request to support
```
Email: info@vukafia.com
Subject: Refund Request - [Business Name] - [Transaction ID]

Message:
- Business Name
- Transaction ID/Receipt
- Payment Date
- Amount
- Detailed reason
- Supporting documentation/screenshots
```

**Step 3**: Support review
- Response within 5 business days
- Investigation if needed
- Decision communicated via email

### 4.2 Refund Timeline

| Decision | Timeline |
|----------|----------|
| Approved | 5-10 business days after approval |
| Denied | 5 business days (with explanation) |
| Requires Investigation | Up to 15 business days |

### 4.3 Refund Method

Refunds are issued to the original payment method:
- **Bachs payments** → Refunded to Bachs account
- **Paystack payments** → Refunded to payment card/method
- **Timeline**: 5-10 business days after approval

## 5. Transaction Disputes (Buyer-Seller)

### 5.1 Vukafia's Role

**Important**: Vukafia does NOT:
- Process transactions between buyers and sellers
- Hold funds in escrow
- Mediate transaction disputes
- Guarantee product/service quality

Vukafia is a **directory platform only**.

### 5.2 Buyer-Seller Disputes

If a customer disputes a purchase with a seller:

1. **Direct Resolution**
   - Contact seller directly via WhatsApp/phone/email
   - Resolve payment/product issues directly
   - Use seller's stated refund policy

2. **Payment Processor Chargeback**
   - If seller won't refund, contact payment processor:
     - **Paystack**: initiate chargeback dispute
     - **Bachs**: file transaction dispute
     - **Bank**: initiate chargeback (if card payment)

3. **Vukafia Involvement**
   - Vukafia may remove fraudulent seller
   - Cannot process refunds on behalf of sellers
   - Can provide dispute mediation (optional)

## 6. Seller Suspension & Refunds

### 6.1 If Seller Is Suspended

- **Claim Fee**: NOT refunded (service was delivered)
- **Active Listing**: Suspended until policy remedied
- **Duration**: 30-90 days (or permanent removal)
- **Appeal**: See Acceptable Use Policy

### 6.2 If Seller Account Is Removed

- **Claim Fee**: NOT refunded (service was delivered before removal)
- **Reason**: Policy violation, fraud, or legal action
- **Data**: Business information deleted from platform
- **Reapplication**: May require 6-12 month waiting period

### 7.1 If Charged Twice

1. **Report Immediately**

   Subject: Duplicate Charge - [Business Name] - [Transaction ID]

2. **Verification**
   - System checks for duplicate transactions

3. **Resolution**
   - Duplicate charge refunded within 3-5 business days
   - Original claim remains active
   - Second business listing created (if requested)

## 10. Chargeback & Fraud Prevention

### 10.1 Chargeback Policy

If you initiate a chargeback with your payment processor:
- Account suspended pending investigation
- Chargeback fee applied ($25-50)
- Additional fraud verification required
- Business removal if chargeback is invalid

### 10.2 Fraud Prevention

Vukafia monitors for:
- Multiple failed payments
- Rapid duplicate payments
- Card/account changes
- Unusual transaction patterns

Suspicious activity may result in account review or suspension.
