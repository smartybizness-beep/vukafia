## 2. Prohibited Business Types

The following business categories are **NOT permitted** on the Vukafia platform:

### Financial Services (Unauthorized)
- ❌ Unlicensed money lending or microcredit
- ❌ Unauthorized forex/currency exchange services
- ❌ Unlicensed investment schemes or trading platforms
- ❌ Pyramid schemes or multi-level marketing (MLM)
- ❌ Unregistered cryptocurrency exchanges
- ❌ Unlicensed insurance services

### Illegal Activities
- ❌ Drug trafficking or sale of controlled substances
- ❌ Weapons/explosives trafficking
- ❌ Human trafficking or forced labor
- ❌ Document fraud or forgery services
- ❌ Money laundering or financial crime services
- ❌ Hacking or cyber crime services

### Prohibited Products & Services
- ❌ Counterfeit goods
- ❌ Stolen property
- ❌ Endangered species or wildlife products
- ❌ Child exploitation material (any form)
- ❌ Adult sexual services
- ❌ Unregistered pharmaceuticals or fake medicines
- ❌ Unlicensed medical procedures

### Fraudulent Operations
- ❌ Advance-fee fraud schemes
- ❌ Romance scams or catfishing services
- ❌ Employment scams
- ❌ Fake documentation services
- ❌ Impersonation of government agencies
- ❌ Fake charity operations

### High-Risk Categories (Require Enhanced Verification)
- ⚠️ Firearms/ammunition (legal only with proper licensing)
- ⚠️ Alcohol/tobacco (must comply with local regulations)
- ⚠️ Gambling services (must be licensed)
- ⚠️ Import/export of regulated goods
- ⚠️ Medical/pharmaceutical services (must be licensed)

## 3. Prohibited Seller Conduct

Sellers must NOT engage in:

### Fraudulent Behavior
- Misrepresenting business information (name, location, credentials)
- Posting fake reviews or ratings
- Impersonating other businesses
- False claims about products/services
- Hidden fees or bait-and-switch pricing

### Harassment & Abuse
- Threatening or abusive communication
- Discrimination based on race, religion, gender, nationality
- Sexual harassment or inappropriate content
- Spam messaging or unsolicited contact
- Doxxing or sharing personal information

### Intellectual Property Violations
- Using trademarked content without permission
- Selling counterfeit branded goods
- Copyright infringement
- Unauthorized use of company logos/branding

### Competitive Abuse
- Creating fake competing listings
- Coordinating negative reviews against competitors
- Spreading false information about competitors
- Unauthorized scraping of competitor data
