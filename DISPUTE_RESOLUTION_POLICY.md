
**Or WhatsApp**: +2348101477935 with same information

**Response Timeline**: 
- Initial acknowledgment: 24 hours
- Detailed response: 5 business days

### 3.2 Complaint Severity Classification

#### 🔴 **Critical/Urgent** (24-48 hour response)
- Fraud or criminal activity
- Harm to person (physical/emotional)
- Illegal activity
- Child safety concerns

#### 🟡 **High Priority** (2-5 business days)
- Policy violation
- Payment dispute
- Significant financial loss
- Harassment or abuse

#### 🟢 **Standard** (5-10 business days)
- Listing accuracy
- Service quality
- Technical issues
- General inquiries

### 3.3 Investigation Phase

**What Vukafia Does**:

1. **Evidence Collection**
   - Request detailed information from complainant
   - Request response from respondent (seller/buyer)

   - Review system logs if technical

2. **Verification**
   - Cross-reference business information with Google Maps data
   - Verify payment records
   - Review communication history
   - Check policy compliance

3. **Neutral Assessment**
   - No bias toward seller or buyer
   - Evidence-based determination
   - Consideration of both parties' statements
   - Policy application verification

**Investigation Timeline**: 5-15 business days depending on complexity

## 4. Resolution Outcomes

### 4.1 Seller Policy Violation

#### **Warning** (Minor Violation)
- First-time minor offense
- Impact: Seller education, warning letter
- Timeline: 5 business days to cure
- Monitoring: 30 days
- **Outcome**: Listing remains active with notice

#### **Suspension** (Serious or Repeated Violation)
- Serious initial violation OR repeated minor violations
- Duration: 30-90 days
- **Outcome**: Listing hidden from directory, seller cannot claim new businesses
- **Right to Appeal**: Yes, within 15 days

#### **Permanent Removal** (Severe/Fraudulent Behavior)
- Fraud, illegal activity, or repeated suspension
- Immediate action
- **Outcome**: Account terminated, listing deleted
- **Claim Fee**: Not refunded
- **Right to Appeal**: Limited (requires extraordinary circumstances)

### 4.2 Buyer-Seller Dispute

#### **No Direct Involvement** (Buyer-Seller Transaction)
- Dispute is between buyer and seller
- Vukafia role: Facilitation only (no involvement in transaction)
- **Outcome**: 
  - Recommend direct communication
  - Provide seller's contact/location
  - Suggest payment processor dispute (if applicable)
  - May mediate if both parties request

#### **Fraud Confirmation** (Seller Scam)
- Seller clearly engaged in fraud
- **Outcome**:
  - Seller account suspended/removed
  - Buyer referred to payment processor for chargeback
  - Law enforcement referral if applicable

#### **Product/Service Quality** (Subjective)
- Dispute over quality (not fraud)
- **Outcome**:
  - No Vukafia action (buyer-seller agreement)
  - Mediation offered (optional)
  - Buyer may escalate to payment processor

### 4.3 Payment/Claim Dispute

#### **Unauthorized Charge**
- Payment made without authorization
- **Outcome**:
  - Refund issued to original payment method
  - Account security review
  - Timeline: 5-10 business days

#### **Duplicate Charge**
- Payment processed twice
- **Outcome**:
  - Duplicate refunded immediately
  - Original claim honored
  - Timeline: 3-5 business days

#### **Failed Payment**
- Payment declined or failed
- **Outcome**:
  - No charge, no listing created
  - Retry recommended
  - Timeline: Immediate

## 5. Appeal Process

### 5.1 Right to Appeal

Users may appeal a Vukafia dispute decision within **15 days** of notification.

**Not Appealable**:
- ❌ Clear fraud or illegal activity
- ❌ Repeatedly violated policies after warnings
- ❌ User appeals already reviewed once

**Appealable**:
- ✅ New evidence emerges
- ✅ Claim factual error in decision
- ✅ Process violation in investigation
- ✅ Disproportionate penalty

### 5.2 Appeal Process

**Step 1**: Submit appeal

```
Email: info@vukafia.com
Subject: APPEAL - [Original Case ID] - [Reason]

Include:
- Case ID/transaction reference
- Reason for appeal
- New evidence (if any)
- Detailed explanation
- Why decision should be reversed
```

**Step 2**: Review by senior staff
- Different reviewer than original
- 7-10 business day review period

**Step 3**: Appeal decision
- ✅ Approved → Original decision reversed
- ⚠️ Partial approval → Modified outcome
- ❌ Denied → Original decision stands (FINAL)

**Timeline**: 10-15 business days total

## Appendix A: Common Dispute Scenarios

**Resolution**: Check if verification pending → Activate listing or refund
**Timeline**: 2-3 days

### Scenario 2: Buyer Complains About Seller Quality
**Issue**: Customer unhappy with purchase
**Resolution**: Not Vukafia's responsibility → Refer to seller directly
**Timeline**: Informational response only

**Issue**: Imposter claiming someone else's business
**Resolution**: Investigate, verify original owner, remove fraudulent claim
**Timeline**: 5-10 days, possible law enforcement referral

**Issue**: Payment failed but deducted from account
**Resolution**: Investigate payment processor, refund if error confirmed
**Timeline**: 3-5 days

**Issue**: Seller/buyer harassing other user
**Resolution**: Warn, suspend communication, or remove account
**Timeline**: 24-48 hours
