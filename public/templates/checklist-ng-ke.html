🇳🇬 NIGERIA - Pre-Export Requirements
Register with Nigerian Customs Service (NCS)
Obtain importer/exporter code. Contact: NCS Tariff and Trade Department
Timeline: 3-5 business days
Obtain Certificate of Origin
Get from the appropriate chamber of commerce (e.g., Nigerian Association of Chambers of Commerce, Industry, Mines and Agriculture)
Timeline: 2-3 business days
Prepare Commercial Invoice
Include: product description, HS codes, quantities, prices, terms of sale, exporter details
Timeline: 1 day
Arrange Pre-shipment Inspection
Contact: COTECNA or COTECNA-accredited inspectors in Nigeria
Timeline: 2-3 business days
Obtain Export License (if applicable)
Contact: Federal Ministry of Trade, Investment and Industry
Timeline: 5-7 business days
Prepare Packing List & Bill of Lading
Detailed documentation of all packages and shipping details
Timeline: 1-2 days
📦 SHIPPING & LOGISTICS
Arrange Freight Forwarder
Select FMDQ-registered forwarder for customs clearance assistance
Timeline: 1-2 days
Arrange Cargo Insurance
Obtain marine cargo insurance covering Lagos to Mombasa/Nairobi route
Timeline: 1 day
File Shipping Documents
Submit documents to shipping company: invoice, packing list, B/L, insurance
Timeline: 1-2 days before shipment
Obtain Bill of Lading
Receive signed B/L from shipping line confirming cargo loaded
Timeline: Same day as loading
🇰🇪 KENYA - Import Requirements
Notify Kenya Revenue Authority (KRA)
Buyer/importer must declare arrival of cargo at port. Contact: KRA Customs
Timeline: Within 14 days of arrival
Customs Entry Procedure
File customs entry form with KRA including: invoice, B/L, packing list, insurance
Timeline: 2-3 business days
Goods Inspection (if required)
KRA may conduct physical inspection at Mombasa or Nairobi port
Timeline: 1-2 business days
Pay Customs Duties & Taxes
Pay applicable import duties and VAT (standard rate: 16% VAT)
Timeline: Before clearance
Obtain Customs Clearance Certificate
Receive C7 form confirming goods cleared through customs
Timeline: After payment and inspection
Collect Goods from Port
Arrange transport from Mombasa Port/JKIA to final destination
Timeline: 1-2 days after clearance
📅 TOTAL COMPLIANCE TIMELINE

Nigeria Pre-Export: 10-15 business days
Shipping: 10-14 days (Lagos → Mombasa)
Kenya Import: 3-5 business days
Total Duration: 25-35 business days (5-7 weeks)
